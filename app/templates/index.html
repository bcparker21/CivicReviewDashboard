Plan Review

The City of Bellevue contracts with SAFEbuilt for building permit plan review services. When an application is submitted, City Staff will do a preliminary review for compliance with Bellevue City Code standards such as setbacks and height. Once Staff has determined that they meet zoning standards, the application is transmitted to the SAFEbuilt plan reviewers.
